# ONLINE performance baseline — 2026-09-15

Raw results: `online-baseline-2026-09-15.json` (generated by
`services/core/benchmarks/run_benchmarks.py`, not hand-written).

## What was measured

- Server: the app image built from this checkout (`docker/app/Dockerfile`),
  one uvicorn worker, PostgreSQL 15 + RDKit cartridge 4.2.0, all ports loopback.
- Dataset: the synthetic demo fixture (`demo-fixture-v1`): 1 family, 3 documents,
  10 compounds, 12 evidence records, 6 measurements, plus the stored target
  investigation for TSLP (69 candidates, 114 measurements).
- 50 samples per read benchmark, 20 per summary/plan benchmark, 10 for the
  executor. p50/p95 in milliseconds; every run reported `errors: 0`.

## What these numbers do and do not say

- They are **regression baselines at fixture scale**, not hosted SLOs. A real
  SureChEMBL package or a public target with thousands of candidates will be
  slower; §20 of AGENTS.md requires the measurement, not a claim.
- Target reads issue **no external request** (the investigation is stored), so
  they are cheap. Live retrieval latency is per source and is recorded in
  `online00-coverage-2026-09-15.json` (`latency_ms` per source: 2–40 s for the
  acceptance targets, dominated by the upstream services).
- **Model latency, token usage and cost are not measured here.** They depend on
  the provider the operator selects and are required before hosted acceptance
  (ONLINE-04/05). Recording them needs a configured endpoint; inventing a number
  from a different provider would be misleading.
- The `open_patent` plan step cannot be benchmarked against the demo fixture,
  because the fixture's synthetic identifiers (`DEMO-PATENT-A`) are deliberately
  not publication-number shaped. The validator is tested against a realistic
  number in `tests/test_online02_plan.py`, and the equivalent read is measured
  through the patent route above.
